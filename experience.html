Software Analysis Lab

Research Intern, Sungkyunkwan Univ.

Designed and evaluated methods for combining two advanced symbolic execution techniques in KLEE: a search strategy and an external parameter tuning strategy. The research proposed three fusion models, Parallel, Balanced, and Alternating, to study whether heterogeneous symbolic execution techniques can improve testing performance when used together.

  • Designed fusion mechanisms that exchange feedback between path selection and parameter tuning modules.
  • Evaluated the models on five GNU C benchmarks with 36,000-second KLEE runs.
  • Showed that Alternating Fusion achieved the best average branch coverage, outperforming KLEE by 131.8%.
  • Found a segmentation fault in combine-0.4.0 that baseline KLEE and the individual strategies missed.
  • Authored and presented the work as first author at KCC2025.
